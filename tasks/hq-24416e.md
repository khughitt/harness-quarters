Why: after hq-61a47e, a git command that writes a new blob of pi/settings.json (checkout, merge, rebase, stash) drops pi's defaultProvider/defaultModel and defaultThinkingLevel, and pi starts on its automatic default. live-picks keeps Claude's and Codex's picks across those rewrites; pi should get the same.

Done: live-picks owns two pi units (defaultProvider + defaultModel; defaultThinkingLevel), saved in local/live-picks.json and restored from the post-checkout, post-merge and post-rewrite hooks; something captures pi's picks at pi's turn end; docs/specs/2026-10-10-live-model-preservation-design.md is amended to cover pi; tests and README follow.

Planned because it amends a reviewed design and its capture point is a choice. Claude and Codex capture at Stop through harness-state-refresh; pi has no hook config, but its extensions receive agent_end, agent_settled (final, notification-only) and session_shutdown (docs/extensions.md in the pi 1.1.0 install). Options to weigh in the spec: an hq-owned pi extension, linked into ~/.pi/agent/extensions/, that runs ~/.local/bin/harness-state-refresh at agent_settled; dispatch through relay once its pi adapter exists (relay-828903); or capture only at Claude/Codex Stop, which misses a pi-only session. Whether lastChangelogVersion needs restoring (pi would show the changelog again) is a question for the spec too.

Where: .githooks/live-picks (OWNED, CLAUDE_GROUPS, FIELD, CLEAN), .githooks/harness-state-refresh, .githooks/test_live_picks.py, the design spec above.
