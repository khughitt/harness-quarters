The user accepted audit hq-7fb9d5 and requested easy monitoring. Current state resets on genuine prompts, disappears at SessionEnd and is pruned after inactivity. Five of seven audited stops preceded an enforced pause, so a hard-pause-only journal is incomplete.

Done: design and implement bounded durable transition history and a read-only hq history/list view with a documented machine-readable handoff for obs. Record warnings, enforced pauses and genuine-user resets/resumes with stable root/turn identity, timestamp, reason, measured counts/limits and notification outcome. Preserve provenance and unknowns. Provide a supported outside-hook route to recognize warning/proactive self-stops from existing task park/provenance and session evidence, without treating every decision park or ordinary approval as a range interruption.

Decide retention, concurrency, restart behavior, failure handling and private-data minimization in the reviewed design. Hook writes stay bounded; correlation/reporting runs outside hooks. Do not log every call into an unbounded stream or introduce a resident service without approval. Portable fixtures cover both harnesses, reset/resume, repeated refusals, self-stops, ordinary gates, missing/partial evidence and notification failure. History survives active-state deletion and distinguishes attempted delivery from receipt. Coordinate schema/fixtures with obs; learned/token/quota alarms remain obs-59789b scope.
