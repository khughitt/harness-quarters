Document the end-to-end procedure and checklist for integrating a new coding-agent harness (such as pi, opencode, or Antigravity CLI) across `hq` and its consumer projects (`lore`, `ops`, `relay`, `tasks`, `obs`).

## Scope & Touchpoints to Document

1. **Home routing & surface delivery (`hq` / `lore`)**
   - Declaring `[harness.<name>]` (and work-home variants) in `links.toml` for global instructions (`lore:instructions/AGENTS.md`), settings, `hooks.json`, and skills (direct `~/.agents/skills` discovery or a manifest like `skills.json`).
   - Handling untracked host/work configs under `local/` and `.ops-check.toml` host-value allowances when required.
   - First-run ordering (`mkdir -p` + `just link --apply` before the harness creates real config files).

2. **Live-state & session-state hygiene (`hq`)**
   - Registering tracked config paths in `.gitattributes` and `.githooks/harness-state-clean` to strip live model/effort picks, installation IDs, and workspace trust from the index.
   - Adding picker groups and record routing in `.githooks/live-picks` and `.githooks/harness-state-refresh`.
   - Preserving workspace trust across Git checkouts (e.g. `.githooks/codex-trust` or a generalized trust helper).

3. **Capability facts & probes (`hq`)**
   - Declaring the harness in `facts/capabilities.toml` (`harnesses = [...]`).
   - Recording `unknown` vs `probed` facts through `tools/harness-facts` and adding reproducible probes under `<harness>/probes/`.

4. **Hooks, profiles & turn-boundary guards (`lore` / `ops`)**
   - Wiring `SessionStart` (`claude-profile`, `capture-notice`) and `Stop` (`claim-guard`, `harness-state-refresh`) hooks, accounting for payload schema differences (snake_case vs camelCase, stdout plain text vs JSON `injectSteps`/`decision`).
   - Extending `ops/bin/ops-profile` (`HARNESS` map and `home_of` transcript/env resolution).

5. **Process ancestry, provenance & live observation (`relay` / `tasks` / `ops`)**
   - Adding a `relay` adapter (`src/adapters/<harness>.js`), hook installer bindings (`src/install/hooks.js`), and question routes (`src/routes/`) if supported.
   - Extending `tasks` process ancestry (`src/relay/ancestry.rs` `HARNESS_COMMS`), native session provenance, and `TASKS_AGENT` / `TASKS_FORMAT` environment injection.
   - Extending `ops/bin/tt` (`agent_of`) for test-run provenance.

6. **Session discovery, archive & transcript readers (`hq` / `obs`)**
   - `hq sessions` discovery and `hq sessions -i` launch flags (`tools/hq_cli/sessions.py`, `tools/hq_cli/launch.py`, `tools/hq.toml`).
   - Transcript capture and pruning sources (`tools/session_archive/config.py`).
   - Episode and session-log readers (`agents/bin/session-episodes` and `obs` adapters).

## Acceptance Criteria

- Runbook added under `docs/runbooks/` (e.g., `docs/runbooks/adding-a-harness.md`) and linked from `README.md`.
- Uses checkout-role placeholders (`<hq-checkout>`, etc.) so `tools/ops-check` passes.
- `just test` and `tasks check` pass.
