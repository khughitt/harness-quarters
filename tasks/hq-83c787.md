Question: What recurring motifs, structural and semantic, do first prompts in interactive Claude Code and Codex sessions fall into? Does "copied" combine with the other motifs as a modifier? Does a session's motif line up with its shape: human-turn count, duration, whether a task is started, whether a spec or plan is written?

Where to start: hq-1459f0 and its source mindful:thought:db1dbdc39a584ca46f0c9a229722c9ff; agents/bin/session-episodes (read_claude, read_codex, and the subagent and non-interactive exclusions); agents/skills/session-logs/SKILL.md, "Human or injected"; obs-82341c's population and exclusion rules for canonical owner turns; the brief docs/notes/2026-10-13-first-prompt-motifs-brief.md.

Bound: Read-only over the last 30 days of the stores. Population: the first human-authored turn of each interactive main-line session. Exclude, and count per reason: subagent transcripts, non-interactive (headless or exec) sessions, resume and fork copies. Count separately the sessions opened by a slash command or skill wrapper, and record their command name, since under the current rule that wrapper is not a human turn. Draw 150 sessions at random, stratified by harness, and split them in half before labelling. On the first 75, label inductively into at most about 10 top-level motifs plus the "copied" modifier, then freeze the taxonomy and record it on this task. Label the second 75 with the frozen set and report the share left as "other". Import the readers from a scratch script outside the repository. No new subcommand, parser, schema change or model judge. Hand labels and verbatim examples stay in a scratch file outside the repository; commit only labels, counts, session locators and paraphrased examples.

Expected result: The taxonomy with definitions and one paraphrased example per motif; counts per motif and per harness, with the exclusion counts; the share marked "copied" and the motifs it combines with; per motif, the distribution of session shape (median human turns, duration, task started yes or no). One recommendation, recorded on this task and in the brief: (1) a session-episodes first-prompts subcommand with deterministic labels, (2) a hand-maintained or judged taxonomy only, after its own judge validation, or (3) stop, because motifs don't separate session shapes. Also list the "copied" sessions that look like relayed Claude-Codex messages, by locator, as candidates for hq-ba1758.
