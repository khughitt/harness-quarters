Why: obs-de84cb (merged in obs 1fcd58c) added `obs index --replay <archive dir> <original root>`, the only way to re-read a transcript after hq prunes its live copy. Its spec makes replay a manual or hq-invoked command; the hourly obs run never replays. Once pruning starts, every obs CURRENT_SCHEMA or EXTRACTORS bump leaves the pruned files rows stale (missing, old stamps) with nothing re-reading them, which is the loss retention policy A exists to prevent.

Done: hq runs `obs index --replay` for each archive source (`<archive root>/<source> <live root>`, five pairs) when obs has bumped its stamps since the last replay, or on a stated cadence after a prune, and records the per-pair report. A `replay_diverged` exit (1) is surfaced as an integrity failure, never retried silently; `unverified` refusals (pre-schema-14 rows) are counted and accepted. The trigger is decided in hq design text first (when to replay, how a stamp change is detected: e.g. obs index-state stamps vs the last replay).

Where: hq session-archive capture/prune service; obs README "Transcript index" (--replay paragraph); obs docs/specs/2026-10-16-archive-replay-design.md §3, §5, §6.
