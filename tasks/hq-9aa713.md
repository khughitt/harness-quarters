The user's ask (2026-10-31): a hook that keeps a session from blowing past the normal range of token usage and turn counts; when it does, the agent pauses and waits for the user to review the situation. For every harness hq wires, not only Codex, where it happened. Agent's reading: a hook reads the session's running counters (model calls, input tokens, active time, compactions, children spawned, and the session tree's totals for a controller) and, past a threshold, blocks further work until the user acknowledges, with a short situation summary. Thresholds start static and move to obs's baselines (obs-59789b). The instruction side is lore's companion task. Case: sun-fbaf80's subagent-driven Codex controller (codex:01a18943-2b9d-7e50-926f-b74169571349) ran from 2026-10-29T20:04Z until the user killed it on 2026-10-31 (~46 h, one turn of ~40 h after the last human message). It made 2,196 model calls on 489M input tokens; the last-30-day median top-level Codex session makes 30 calls on 2.9M (p99 709 and 129M, from obs's index). It compacted 11 times (p90 1), spawned 109 subagents (69 of them reviews or re-reviews) and left the Codex weekly rate limit at 95%. The controller spent 25.3 h in its own inference and 20.6 h in wait_agent; of its 1,134 exec calls, 467 re-read ledgers and plans and 308 recomputed sha256s. Children: 26.8 h model, 10.6 h tools. Tests (tt runs.jsonl): 87 test-fast runs, 6.8 h; 330 test-one, 2.0 h. A live catalog acquisition took ~5 h; three resumes ended in fail: acquire. The run overrode the P0 halt sun-14f4c4 nine times on its own authority. Profiled read-only from the rollouts by claude-code:1219a183-f486-4832-ac92-170d315e5fc2; detailed case on obs-59789b.
