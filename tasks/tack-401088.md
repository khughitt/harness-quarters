Adopted policy (docs/notes/2026-09-29-session-store-retention-brief.md, Decision, 2026-09-30): archive, then prune. Daily incremental capture of both Claude homes (~/.claude, ~/.claude-work; transcripts with subagents/ and tool-results/) and Codex sessions/ and archived_sessions/ to /mnt/backup; monthly compression and prune of files inactive for 30 days and unchanged since capture, only when a readable archive holds the exact version obs indexed; Codex threads removed with codex delete --force; on backup unavailability or verification failure keep the originals and report. Running sessions are never eligible; restoring into live harness directories is not part of the workflow. Archive re-read is the rollout acceptance check, owned by obs-de84cb. Planned: design spec, then plan, before implementation.
