~/.codex holds 31G on the root filesystem (sessions 21G, packages 4.8G, thread_history 2.6G, archived_sessions 1.6G, logs 1.3G) and ~/.claude 1.6G; a 10G tarball of both from 2026-08-30 also sits on a second disk. Nothing ages any of it. Decide what a session store is for after N days (provenance, resumability, nothing), then either prune, archive to /mnt/backup, or relocate the stores off root with the same host-local pattern as CACHE_DIR.
