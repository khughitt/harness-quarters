hq-d05d5c made rename-cutover refuse any snapshot repository whose records live outside it (a detached project), since rollback resets only the repositories it saves and tasks cannot re-point a detached checkout after the checkout directory moves (tasks-e9ce63). Supporting a detached rename means: snapshot the records repository (head, branch, clean, one worktree, no stashes or unmerged branches), treat its <new>/tasks leftovers as rename leftovers at the prefix offset, reset it on rollback, re-point the moved checkout once tasks can, and sample verify's record under the new records directory. Records-home design §4 and §5.4 (tasks repository) expect this support.
