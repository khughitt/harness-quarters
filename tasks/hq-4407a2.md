Why: When `ponytail@ponytail` is enabled in Codex (`codex/config.toml`, `local/codex/config.work.toml`) and Claude Code work (`local/claude/settings.work.json`) without `~/.config/ponytail/config.json`, `hooks/ponytail-config.js` defaults `defaultMode` to `"full"`. On every `SessionStart` (`ponytail-activate.js`) and `SubagentStart` (`ponytail-subagent.js`), the plugin then injects `skills/ponytail/SKILL.md` as a late `role: developer` message after `AGENTS.md`, including the unconditional directive `End your reply with one or two lines: what you skipped or did not check, and any risk the user must know.` Upstream Ponytail 5.3.3 has no config key to strip only the footer while keeping `defaultMode: "full"`, but supports `"defaultMode": "off"` in `~/.config/ponytail/config.json` to disable automatic `SessionStart`/`SubagentStart` hook injection while keeping the plugin and all six skills (`ponytail`, `ponytail-review`, `ponytail-audit`, `ponytail-debt`, `ponytail-gain`, `ponytail-help`) installed and available in the skill catalog.

Done: `ponytail/config.json` (`{"defaultMode": "off"}`) is tracked in `hq` and linked at `~/.config/ponytail/config.json` under `[required]` in `links.toml`, with `tools/test_harness_links.py` and `README.md` updated and passing `just test-one tools/test_harness_links.py` and `just check-hygiene`.

Where: `ponytail/config.json`, `links.toml`, `tools/test_harness_links.py`, `README.md`.
